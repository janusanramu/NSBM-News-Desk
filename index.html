NSBM 6th Student Research Symposium in Marketing, Tourism and Multimedia

PP Ms. Anne Pathiranage, Dean (Acting)
Dean, Faculty of Business

NSBM Green University successfully hosted the 6th Student Research Symposium in Marketing, Tourism and Multimedia (SRSMTM 2026) on 8 October 2026, organised by the Department of Marketing and Tourism Management, Faculty of Business. The event brought together academic staff and undergraduates to celebrate research, innovation, and knowledge sharing.

A Place to Turn Ideas into Startups

Held under the theme “Intelligent Futures: Integrating Creativity, Communication, Marketing and Experience in the AI Age,” the symposium explored the evolving role of artificial intelligence in shaping marketing, tourism, communication, and multimedia practices.

"The symposium featured more than 20 undergraduate research papers, showcasing the research interests, analytical capabilities, and innovative perspectives of NSBM undergraduates. A keynote address by Prof. Ravi Dissanayake of the University of Kelaniya further enriched the academic programme, offering valuable insights into the symposium’s central theme."

“We want our students to start their own ventures, not only look for jobs after graduation.”

— Ms. Anne Pathiranage, Dean (Acting), Faculty of Business

Mentoring, Funding and Industry Support

SRSMTM 2026 provided a platform for students to present their research, exchange ideas, and engage with contemporary developments across their fields of study. The event also reinforced the Faculty of Business’s commitment to fostering research engagement and encouraging students to explore emerging trends and challenges in an increasingly technology-driven world.

The faculty is also talking with local companies and startup networks about internships and joint projects, which could help students build real industry contacts early.

NSBM Opens Rs. 720 Million Civil Engineering Laboratory Complex

PF Dr. Chandana Perera
Dean, Faculty of Engineering

NSBM Green University ceremonially opened its state-of-the-art Civil Engineering Laboratory Complex, representing a significant Rs. 720 million self-financed investment in engineering education, on 26 October 2026 as part of its 10th anniversary celebrations. Located within the university’s Phase II development at Pitipana, Homagama, the new complex marks an important milestone in NSBM’s continued investment in modern academic infrastructure and practical engineering education. Equipped with modern infrastructure, high-tech laboratories and advanced learning facilities, the complex is designed to strengthen students’ practical learning experiences and support the development of industry-relevant engineering skills.

Hands-on Learning with Industrial Automation

The complex was ceremonially declared open by the Attorney General of Sri Lanka, Hon. K. A. Parinda Ranasinghe Jr., PC, in the distinguished presence of NSBM Founder and Vice-Chancellor Prof. E. A. Weerasinghe; Prof. Richard Davies, Vice-Chancellor and CEO of the University of Plymouth, United Kingdom; Prof. Tyrone Carlin, Vice-Chancellor and President of Southern Cross University, Australia; and Prof. Li Lu, Vice-Chancellor and President of Zhejiang Shuren University, China.

“We want our students to build things, break things and fix them here, so they are ready for industry on day one.”

— Dr. Chandana Perera, Faculty of Engineering

Built for Student Projects and Industry Links

The Rs. 720 million investment reflects NSBM’s commitment to providing students with an academic environment that connects theoretical knowledge with practical application. By strengthening its engineering facilities, the university aims to equip future engineers with the technical knowledge, practical experience and competencies required to meet the evolving demands of the profession

PF

About Dr. Chandana Perera

Dr. Chandana Perera is the Dean of the Faculty of Engineering at NSBM Green University, with over 20 years of experience in industrial automation and mechatronics.

NSBM Green University Celebrates 10 Years of Excellence and National Impact

MW Founder and Vice Chancellor Prof. E. A. Weerasinghe
Head, Student Affairs

NSBM Green University celebrated its 10th anniversary on 26 October 2026 with a grand ceremony at the NSBM Auditorium in Homagama, marking a decade of achievements, growth and contributions to Sri Lankan higher education. Held at the invitation of Founder and Vice Chancellor Prof. E. A. Weerasinghe and the Board of Directors, the ceremony brought together distinguished local and international guests, including diplomatic representatives, government officials, industry partners, alumni and leaders from NSBM’s international university partnerships.

Among the distinguished guests were the Vice-Chancellors of the University of Plymouth, United Kingdom, and Southern Cross University, Australia; the Executive President of Zhejiang Shuren University, China; and Assoc. Prof. Ranjith Ihalanayake, Dean and Head of Programme at the Business School of Victoria University, Australia. A key highlight of the celebration was the formal exchange of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between NSBM Green University and Zhejiang Shuren University, China, marking another step towards strengthening international academic collaboration and expanding opportunities for global engagement.

NSBM Unveils Model of Proposed Multibillion Medical and Research Hospital

NSBM Green University unveiled the architectural model of its proposed multibillion-rupee Medical and Research Hospital on 26 October 2026, marking a significant milestone in the university’s 10th anniversary celebrations and its strategic expansion into healthcare and medical research.

Planned for a 20-acre site opposite the NSBM Green University premises in Pitipana, Homagama, the proposed hospital is a flagship development under Phase III of the university’s expansion programme. The initiative reflects NSBM’s ambition to extend its contribution beyond higher education into healthcare, medical research and interdisciplinary innovation as it embarks on its next decade under NSBM NEXT.

"The proposed multispecialty hospital is envisioned as a modern healthcare facility serving both local and international communities. Its planned scope encompasses advanced inpatient care, outpatient care, advanced diagnostic services, wellness services, research-based healthcare, international patient services and smart healthcare technologies.

By bringing healthcare services and research together, the proposed development aims to create opportunities for collaboration across healthcare, science, technology and education. It also represents a significant step in NSBM’s broader vision of exploring new avenues for national development through strategic investment and innovation.

The unveiling of the architectural model during the university’s tenth anniversary celebrations offered a glimpse into this future development, highlighting NSBM’s long-term plans to diversify its institutional footprint and contribute to an evolving healthcare landscape in Sri Lanka. As NSBM moves into its next decade, the proposed Medical and Research Hospital stands as a key component of its future vision under NSBM NEXT, reflecting its ambition to create new opportunities, strengthen research capabilities and broaden its contribution to the country and the wider international community."

“The proposed multispecialty hospital is envisioned as a modern healthcare facility serving both local and international communities.”

— Founder and Vice Chancellor Prof. E. A. Weerasinghe

New Members and Upcoming Activities

NSBM Healthcare Limited is currently in the process of developing a state-of-the-art, multi-speciality hospital in Pitipana, Homagama. The hospital is designed to meet the medical and clinical needs of both local and international patients, offering advanced diagnostic, therapeutic, and surgical care across a wide range of specialities. It will also function as a teaching and research hospital, providing essential clinical training for students of NSBM’s proposed Faculty of Medicine and allied health programs, while fostering an environment for innovative medical research

NSBM Cricket Team Crowned Champions Again at KDU Inter-University T20 Championship 2026

CD Coach Dias
Director, Sports Council

NSBM Green University has once again made its mark in university cricket, with the NSBM Cricket Team securing the KDU Leaders’ Trophy Inter-University T20 Cricket Championship 2026, marking its second consecutive Inter-University Cricket Championship following its historic championship victory last year.

A Strong Show Across Many Events

Maintaining an exceptional run throughout the tournament, NSBM emerged as the only unbeaten team, demonstrating remarkable consistency, discipline and determination across the competition. In the final against SLIIT, NSBM delivered a commanding all-round performance. An outstanding bowling display, led by Punsara Rajakaruna, who claimed 3 wickets for 23 runs, restricted SLIIT to 130/9 in their allotted 20 overs.

“These students trained before lectures and after lectures for months, and today all that effort has paid off.”

— Coach Dias, Sports Council

Support for Student Athletes

The Cricket Council said the university will continue to support its players with better training facilities, nutrition guidance and flexible exam schedules during match season. Selections for the next season will be open to all students, including first year students who have not played for the university before.

The team will now start preparing for the national level tournaments later this year, and the Cricket Council has asked interested students to register for the training camps through the student portal.

CD

About Coach Dias

Coach Dias is the Director of the Cricket Council at NSBM Green University, with over 15 years of experience in cricket coaching and university sports management.

End Semester Examination Timetable Released for All Faculties

EO Examinations Office
NSBM Green University

The Examinations Office has released the end semester examination timetable for all faculties. Students are advised to check their exam dates, times and venues carefully and to read the instructions below before the examinations begin.

Timetable and Admission Details

The examinations will begin on the first Monday of next month and continue for three weeks. Individual timetables are now available on the student portal under the Examinations section. Students must collect their admission cards from the portal and bring a printed copy, along with their student ID, to every exam. Students without a valid admission card or student ID will not be allowed into the examination hall.

"Please check your timetable early and report any clashes immediately," said an official from the Examinations Office. "Clashes reported after the deadline cannot be accepted."

“Please check your timetable early and report any clashes immediately.”

— Examinations Office, NSBM Green University

Important Instructions for Students

Students should arrive at the venue at least thirty minutes before the exam starts. Mobile phones, smart watches and any unauthorised material are strictly prohibited inside the hall. Students who need special arrangements or want to report a timetable clash must contact the Examinations Office through the student portal before the deadline. Students with pending fees or attendance issues should clear them with the relevant offices as soon as possible.

The Examinations Office wishes all students the very best for their exams. Any further updates or changes will be announced on the student portal and on NSBM NewsDesk.

EO

About the Examinations Office

The Examinations Office at NSBM Green University manages the planning, scheduling and conduct of all university examinations, and handles results, re-corrections and student examination queries.
