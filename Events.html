NSBM Green University Celebrates 10 Years of Excellence and National Impact

MW Founder and Vice Chancellor Prof. E. A. Weerasinghe
Head, Student Affairs

NSBM Green University celebrated its 10th anniversary on 26 October 2026 with a grand ceremony at the NSBM Auditorium in Homagama, marking a decade of achievements, growth and contributions to Sri Lankan higher education. Held at the invitation of Founder and Vice Chancellor Prof. E. A. Weerasinghe and the Board of Directors, the ceremony brought together distinguished local and international guests, including diplomatic representatives, government officials, industry partners, alumni and leaders from NSBM’s international university partnerships.

Among the distinguished guests were the Vice-Chancellors of the University of Plymouth, United Kingdom, and Southern Cross University, Australia; the Executive President of Zhejiang Shuren University, China; and Assoc. Prof. Ranjith Ihalanayake, Dean and Head of Programme at the Business School of Victoria University, Australia. A key highlight of the celebration was the formal exchange of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between NSBM Green University and Zhejiang Shuren University, China, marking another step towards strengthening international academic collaboration and expanding opportunities for global engagement.

NSBM Unveils Model of Proposed Multibillion Medical and Research Hospital

NSBM Green University unveiled the architectural model of its proposed multibillion-rupee Medical and Research Hospital on 26 October 2026, marking a significant milestone in the university’s 10th anniversary celebrations and its strategic expansion into healthcare and medical research.

Planned for a 20-acre site opposite the NSBM Green University premises in Pitipana, Homagama, the proposed hospital is a flagship development under Phase III of the university’s expansion programme. The initiative reflects NSBM’s ambition to extend its contribution beyond higher education into healthcare, medical research and interdisciplinary innovation as it embarks on its next decade under NSBM NEXT.

"The proposed multispecialty hospital is envisioned as a modern healthcare facility serving both local and international communities. Its planned scope encompasses advanced inpatient care, outpatient care, advanced diagnostic services, wellness services, research-based healthcare, international patient services and smart healthcare technologies.

By bringing healthcare services and research together, the proposed development aims to create opportunities for collaboration across healthcare, science, technology and education. It also represents a significant step in NSBM’s broader vision of exploring new avenues for national development through strategic investment and innovation.

The unveiling of the architectural model during the university’s tenth anniversary celebrations offered a glimpse into this future development, highlighting NSBM’s long-term plans to diversify its institutional footprint and contribute to an evolving healthcare landscape in Sri Lanka. As NSBM moves into its next decade, the proposed Medical and Research Hospital stands as a key component of its future vision under NSBM NEXT, reflecting its ambition to create new opportunities, strengthen research capabilities and broaden its contribution to the country and the wider international community."

“The proposed multispecialty hospital is envisioned as a modern healthcare facility serving both local and international communities.”

— Founder and Vice Chancellor Prof. E. A. Weerasinghe

New Members and Upcoming Activities

NSBM Healthcare Limited is currently in the process of developing a state-of-the-art, multi-speciality hospital in Pitipana, Homagama. The hospital is designed to meet the medical and clinical needs of both local and international patients, offering advanced diagnostic, therapeutic, and surgical care across a wide range of specialities. It will also function as a teaching and research hospital, providing essential clinical training for students of NSBM’s proposed Faculty of Medicine and allied health programs, while fostering an environment for innovative medical research
