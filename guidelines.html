Community Guidelines

A shared space for respectful campus conversations

Project draft: This is suggested policy text for the NSBM NewsDesk project, not an approved or official NSBM University policy. Please have your project team review it before publication.

check_circle Be respectful

Treat students, staff, clubs, and contributors with courtesy. Disagree with ideas without insulting or targeting people.

check_circle Share accurate information

Check facts before posting. Clearly separate opinions from verified news, and correct mistakes when you discover them.

check_circle Protect privacy

Do not publish someone else’s personal contact details, private messages, or images without appropriate permission.

check_circle Keep content relevant

Post campus-related news, events, club updates, and constructive discussions. Avoid spam, repetitive promotions, and misleading headlines.

check_circle Respect original work

Credit authors and photographers. Only upload writing, images, and media that you have permission to share.

check_circle Report concerns responsibly

Use available reporting channels or contact the NewsDesk team if you see harmful, misleading, or inappropriate content.

check_circle Moderation

Content may be reviewed or removed when it conflicts with these guidelines. Repeated violations may lead to account restrictions, subject to the site's moderation process.
