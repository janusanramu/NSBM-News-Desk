Terms of Service

Using NSBM NewsDesk responsibly

Project draft: This is suggested policy text for the NSBM NewsDesk project, not an approved or official NSBM University policy. Please have your project team review it before publication.

check_circle Using the platform

NSBM NewsDesk is intended for campus news, student contributions, club updates, and community engagement. Use it lawfully and respectfully.

check_circle Accounts and eligibility

Provide accurate registration details, keep your login credentials private, and notify the site team if you suspect unauthorized access.

check_circle Your submissions

You remain responsible for articles, comments, and media you submit. Only share material you own or have permission to use.

check_circle Content visibility

Submitted material may be reviewed before publication. Published posts may be visible to other users of the platform.

check_circle Acceptable use

Do not attempt to disrupt the service, impersonate others, upload malicious material, or misuse other users’ information.

check_circle Changes and availability

Features and content may change as the project develops. Access may occasionally be interrupted for maintenance or technical reasons.

check_circle Questions and contact

For questions about site rules or your account, contact the NewsDesk team at newsdesk@nsbm.ac.lk.
