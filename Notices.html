End Semester Examination Timetable Released for All Faculties

EO Examinations Office
NSBM Green University

The Examinations Office has released the end semester examination timetable for all faculties. Students are advised to check their exam dates, times and venues carefully and to read the instructions below before the examinations begin.

Timetable and Admission Details

The examinations will begin on the first Monday of next month and continue for three weeks. Individual timetables are now available on the student portal under the Examinations section. Students must collect their admission cards from the portal and bring a printed copy, along with their student ID, to every exam. Students without a valid admission card or student ID will not be allowed into the examination hall.

"Please check your timetable early and report any clashes immediately," said an official from the Examinations Office. "Clashes reported after the deadline cannot be accepted."

“Please check your timetable early and report any clashes immediately.”

— Examinations Office, NSBM Green University

Important Instructions for Students

Students should arrive at the venue at least thirty minutes before the exam starts. Mobile phones, smart watches and any unauthorised material are strictly prohibited inside the hall. Students who need special arrangements or want to report a timetable clash must contact the Examinations Office through the student portal before the deadline. Students with pending fees or attendance issues should clear them with the relevant offices as soon as possible.

The Examinations Office wishes all students the very best for their exams. Any further updates or changes will be announced on the student portal and on NSBM NewsDesk.

EO

About the Examinations Office

The Examinations Office at NSBM Green University manages the planning, scheduling and conduct of all university examinations, and handles results, re-corrections and student examination queries.
