# My Submissions – NSBM News Desk

## Overview

The **My Submissions** page is part of the Student/Author Portal in the NSBM News Desk web application.

This page provides a clean and user-friendly interface for students to manage their submitted news articles, track publication progress, and view their submission history.

The interface follows the NSBM News Desk green-themed design, focusing on simplicity, accessibility, and an organized user experience.

## Features

### 1. Submission Dashboard

The page displays four summary cards:

- **Total Submissions** – Total number of articles submitted by the student.
- **Published / Approved** – Articles successfully approved or published.
- **Pending Review** – Articles awaiting editorial review.
- **Needs Revision** – Articles requiring changes or improvements.

### 2. Article History

The Article History section displays submitted articles with relevant information, including:

- Article thumbnail image
- Article title
- Short article description
- Article category
- Submission date
- Publication status

### 3. Article Management

Each article includes management options:

- **Edit** – Access the article editing option.
- **Delete** – Access the article deletion option.
- **New Submission** – Navigate to the article submission process.

### 4. Status Filtering

A status filter is provided to help students organize and locate submissions according to their publication status.

### 5. Pagination

The pagination interface organizes article records and provides navigation between pages.

## User Interface Design

The My Submissions page uses a modern dashboard layout with:

- NSBM-inspired green color theme
- Clean typography and consistent spacing
- Summary cards with status icons
- Article thumbnails and status indicators
- Clearly organized action buttons
- Structured article history section
- Simple and intuitive navigation

The NSBM News Desk project uses the following web technologies:

- **HTML5** – Page structure and content

## Purpose

The main objective of this page is to provide students with a centralized platform to monitor and manage their news article submissions.

It helps improve the overall submission experience by presenting article information, review status, and management options in one organized interface.
